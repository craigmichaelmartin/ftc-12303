# R7 Robot audit: fact-check

R7 had no fact-check when it was written. This file is that fact-check. It was done on 2026-10-20 as part of the audit of all 12303 deliverables (plan docs, playbook, code). Each R7 claim was checked against:
- the TU05 manual (text, plus PDF p.73 and p.133 checked visually; p.124–125 for R105);
- the Inspection Checklist 26-27.1 (10/09/26) and the Inspection Quick Reference;
- live manufacturer pages (goBILDA, REV, SWYFT), checked 2026-10-20;
- new crops of the three robot photos.

The audit's scratch files (session scratchpad, not in the repo): photo crops in `scratchpad/audit/photos/r7/`, calculations in `scratchpad/audit/scripts/r7_check.py`, Inspection Checklist text in `scratchpad/audit/r7/ic.txt`.

Verdicts: **confirmed**; **corrected** (partly wrong, corrected statement given); **refuted** (wrong as written); **measure on robot** (the photos can't settle it, so measure it on the robot).

**Result:** 27 rows: 10 confirmed, 12 corrected, 1 refuted, 4 measure on robot. Nearly all of R7's part identifications, manufacturer specs and rule citations hold. The problems are in some inferences and in how they reached the plan. **R7 itself is not edited: read it together with this table. Where this table corrects R7, this table wins** (the same rule as the other `*.verify.md` files).

Line numbers refer to `R7-robot-audit.md` as committed on 2026-10-20.

## Where the corrections went

- **Plan docs:** 05 §1 (blockers: grounding strap recommended, team-reachable switch, R105 through the full mechanical travel, servo count), §3.2–3.5 (flywheel fit, hood contact surface, bevel drive and `FLYWHEEL_TICKS_PER_REV`, conditional reprints, turret options with the encoder on an idler gear, calibrated zero, `TURRET_PINNED` fallback), §4 (intake motor sizing), §5–§7 (servo budget, shopping list and totals, measure-on-robot questions); 00 (week 1–2 tasks, risk 6); 07 set-up checks ("measure on robot" rows); 08 pit checklist (turret zero, not "homes").
- **Strategy brief:** robot description (turret travel, bevel drive, intake layout to measure) and the robot refinements (strap recommended, 5-turn travel, encoder as feedback, intake motor sizing).
- **Playbook** (the claude.ai page; repo copy [`docs/playbook/biobuzz-playbook.html`](../playbook/biobuzz-playbook.html#robot)): the Robot section's "Refinements, in priority order" table (grounding strap recommended, not required; battery cradle not an inspection item; a measure-on-robot row for the ring and pinion teeth, the bevel/miter drive and `FLYWHEEL_TICKS_PER_REV` = 28 × ratio, the hood contact surface, the intake roller layout and the 60 mm flywheel fit; turret travel about 50–75° as built and 300–450° with the 5-turn servo, the encoder on an idler gear for feedback only, never continuous mode, a calibrated zero and no home switch, the `TURRET_PINNED` fallback; an intake motor at 435 or 312 rpm, chosen after measuring the roller).
- **Code** (2026-10-20): `README-12303.md` §2 (encoder on an idler gear, feedback only, never continuous mode; no home switch; `intake` may be a motor), `RobotConfig` comments and defaults (`FLYWHEEL_TICKS_PER_REV` = 28 × ratio, `TURRET_GEAR_RATIO` about 4–6 with the soft-limit formulas, `TURRET_ENCODER_TICKS_PER_DEG` placeholder with calibrate-first, encoder re-zero, `ACCEPT_OWN_NECTAR = false`).
